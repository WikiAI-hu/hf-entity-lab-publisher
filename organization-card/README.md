# WikiAI.hu

**Human-centered information. Machine-readable knowledge. Clearer digital identities.**

## About WikiAI

**WikiAI.hu** is a Hungarian editorial business-knowledge platform launched on 8 September 2026. Its purpose is to help people, search engines and AI-assisted information services more accurately interpret information about real businesses, services, professionals and their publicly verifiable relationships.

The platform combines editorial work, practical website development, structured information and technical documentation. Its approach prioritizes **people-first content** while making source relationships, professional identities and business facts easier for machines to interpret.

WikiAI.hu is operated by **Várhelyi Viktória**, a Hungarian sole proprietor. **Csanád Várhelyi (Várhelyi Csanád)** serves as responsible editor and a lead professional author. The WikiAI platform and the editor's independent research publications are distinct, but related, activities.

## Focus Areas

- **LLM-first website architecture:** organizing websites around understandable entities, evidence and clearly defined information relationships.
- **Semantic web and structured data:** Schema.org, JSON-LD, entity references and consistent public profiles.
- **Knowledge graphs and entity engineering:** documenting connections between organizations, people, places, services and publications.
- **Search and AI interpretability:** SEO, AEO, GEO and LLMO approaches that emphasize information quality over ranking claims.
- **Digital provenance and trust:** source attribution, traceability, transparent editorial context and reviewable technical documentation.
- **Applied business knowledge:** documenting the actual activities and services of Hungarian enterprises in a structured, human-readable way.

## Working Principles

1. **Accuracy before visibility:** descriptions of businesses and professionals should reflect their real-world activities.
2. **People before algorithms:** information must remain useful and understandable to human readers.
3. **Traceable sources:** links and identifiers should lead to genuine, relevant supporting material.
4. **Privacy and consent:** no confidential customer information or private datasets are published as research assets without appropriate authorization.
5. **No guaranteed rankings:** structured data and public research records do not guarantee search positions, AI recommendations or Google Knowledge Panels.
